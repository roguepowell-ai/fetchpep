# Product

> **Partly written.** From George's decisions of 24 Sep and his design boards (the Inkfold
> design system). The rest was worked out in planning and never written down — O-18.

## The game and the website are separate — D-045

Two separate experiences, specified and built separately. **This repo's work is the game.**
The website — claiming a fold, inviting members, submitting drawings, screening — is its
own experience and gets its own spec when it is built.

Do not describe the game in terms of the website, or draw them as one pipeline. That
mistake was made three times on 24 Sep; the record is in `state/SUPERSEDED.state.md`.

## The game — D-045

**The core is exploring and encounters.** Players walk the world map — world, continent,
country, area, locality, fold (D-053) — and meet creatures there: capture, satchel and
crafting, the pen, visitors, the field guide, quests and the fair.

The word *region* is not used in the game (D-051).

## Creatures — D-058

The game holds **a catalogue of creatures**. Where a creature came from does not matter to
the game. What it needs is its credentials:

```
  CREATURE (in the catalogue)          CAPTURE (when a member catches one)
  ─────────────────────────            ──────────────────────────────────
  artist        who drew it            creature      which one
  creator       who created it         caught by     the member
  + sprite, habitat, rarity            caught at     the capture time
```

**Pilot:** every creature is seeded. Artist and creator are both **Joshua**.

Credits shown to players are tags, never real names (D-056).

## Players — D-054

The steward and every member each have their own login and identity. One age-verified
steward per fold; members join a fold by invite. Sign-in methods are open — O-47.

## What is on record

Members and stewards, one age-verified adult steward per fold with up to eight invited
members. No chat, no friends, no follows. Quests come from NPCs. Private folds are never
drawn. The pilot is a single shard and ships free.
