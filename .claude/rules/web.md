# Steward website

Routes to `spec/SCREENING.spec.md`, `spec/BRAND.spec.md` and `spec/IDENTITY.spec.md`.
Does not restate them.

## What the website is

Administration and submission. The app is the game; the website is everything else —
submitting a drawing, the steward gate, account and payment. [certain] Gibraltar cannot
register as a Google Play merchant, so revenue is web-side via Stripe only.

## Submissions

- The original upload is stored **untouched at archival resolution**. Screening works on a
  derived copy. The original is the artwork and is never the working file.
- The drawing is shown **full size** at the gate. A decision made on a thumbnail is not a
  decision.
- Pre-screening produces four outcomes, and uncertainty routes to a person. An automated
  rejection that cannot be appealed is a rejection nobody reviewed.

## Copy

Every string here is read by a person, so `spec/BRAND.spec.md` applies to all of it.
Rejections never shame and never specify.
